2.5.0 is a big one. I've been moving Aurral toward a proper music library, while keeping Discovery focused on finding and acquiring music.

## What's new

- A native Library for browsing, organizing, and playing owned music.
- Clearer paths between Discovery and Library. Discovery handles previews and acquisition, while Library handles full playback and owned-music actions.
- Hosted Subsonic support for browsing and playing Aurral's library from compatible clients.
- Flow tracks can be kept, promoted into the Library, and added to permanent playlists.
- Local listening history and scrobbling.
- Better playlist imports, syncing, download matching, and playback cleanup.

## Things to try

- Open Library and play an artist, album, or track directly.
- Open an owned release from Discovery, then use Library for full playback.
- Favorite a Flow track and check that it is kept in your Library.
- Connect a Subsonic-compatible client to Aurral and browse or play your music.
- Import a playlist from Spotify, ListenBrainz, or Last.fm.

## Full change list

I've kept this release note focused on the changes you are most likely to notice. The [full change list from 2.4.0 to 2.5.0](https://github.com/lklynet/aurral/compare/v2.4.0...v2.5.0) has every pull request and fix.

## What's next

A future release will tackle the separate Lidarr and Aurral ownership modes. That includes split-root validation, fresh setup and migration checks, and support for media that does not have embedded MusicBrainz IDs.

After that, I'll continue the Aurral-owned writes, monitoring, acquisition, and optional Lidarr monitoring work. Those pieces are intentionally not part of this release.

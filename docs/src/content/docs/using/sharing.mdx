---
title: Sharing
description: Send an artist, album, or track to friends without exposing your server.
---

You can share an artist, album, or track with anyone, even if your Aurral is only on your home network.

- **Artist:** open the artist page, select the more actions button, then **Share artist**.
- **Album:** open the album page and select the share button.
- **Track:** while a track plays, select the share button in the player.

On a phone, or a browser with a share menu, Aurral opens that menu so you can pick an app. Otherwise it copies the link. If the browser blocks copying, which can happen when you open Aurral over plain `http://`, select **Open link** and copy the address from the new tab.

## What your friend sees

The link opens a page on aurral.org with the cover art, the title, and links to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, TIDAL, and Deezer. A track can also have a 30-second preview from Deezer. Messaging apps show the art and title in the link preview.

If your friend runs Aurral too, they can select **Open in my Aurral** to go to the same artist or album on their own server and add it there. Their browser remembers their Aurral address, and it is never sent to aurral.org.

## What a link contains

A share link holds only the item's name, its artist and album names, and its MusicBrainz IDs. It contains nothing about you or your server: no address, username, file path, or library contents. Anyone with the link can read those names, so treat it like any message you send.

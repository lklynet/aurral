---
title: Inbox
description: Library-based releases, shows, news, and discoveries in Aurral.
---

The Inbox is the bell icon beside your profile menu. It is a compact update feed for changes that need your attention.

Inbox items are private to each Aurral user. The source library is shared, but read and dismissed state is not.

## What appears

- Upcoming and recently released albums from artists in the Lidarr library
- Upcoming shows for library artists when Ticketmaster is configured
- Optional library-artist news from RSS feeds
- Optional recommended-artist news from RSS feeds
- Personalized artist discoveries from Discover

The green Inbox icon shows that unread items exist. Select an item to mark it as read; it stays in the Inbox with reduced emphasis. Use the item actions to save, dismiss, or mark it as added. Artist and release items open their full pages, where you can decide whether to add them to Lidarr. Use the filter button to show all notifications or one category. The dropdown scrolls when there are more updates than fit on screen.

## Settings

Admins can enable or disable each Inbox category in the **Inbox** section below **Notification Events** under **Settings > Connect**. Only library-artist news and recommended-artist news are available as news Inbox categories. Configure RSS feeds and feed groups under **Settings > RSS News**. Ticketmaster is configured under **Settings > Connect > Ticketmaster**.

News is optional. Enable it under **Settings > RSS News**. This page lists Aurral's built-in RSS feeds, lets you enable or disable feed groups, and lets you add or remove custom feeds. Aurral combines enabled feeds locally, matches stories to library and recommended artist names, and applies publisher blocks before creating Inbox items.

Inbox data refreshes in the background once a day and also refreshes when opened. Artist news advances in the background every six hours and checks due artists when the news page opens.

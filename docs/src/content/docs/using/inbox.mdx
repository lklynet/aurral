---
title: Inbox
description: Library-based releases, shows, news, and discoveries in Aurral.
---

The Inbox is the bell-shaped icon beside your profile menu. It is a compact update feed, not a second Discover page.

Inbox items are private to each Aurral user. The source library is shared, but read and dismissed state is not.

## What appears

- Upcoming and recently released albums from artists in the Lidarr library
- Upcoming shows for library artists when Ticketmaster is configured
- Optional daily artist-news digests from NewsAPI
- Personalized artist discoveries from Discover

The green Inbox icon shows that unread items exist. Select an item to mark it as read; it stays in the Inbox with reduced emphasis. Select the checkmark to remove it. Artist and release items open their full pages, where you can decide whether to add them to Lidarr. Use the filter button to show all notifications or one category. The dropdown scrolls when there are more updates than fit on screen.

## Settings

Admins can enable or disable each Inbox category under **Settings > Connect > Inbox**. Ticketmaster is configured under **Settings > Connect > Ticketmaster**.

News is optional. Add a NewsAPI key under **Settings > Connect > NewsAPI**. Aurral stores the key encrypted with the instance key and sends NewsAPI requests from the server. Publisher blocks from Artist News also apply to Inbox news. Aurral checks up to 25 due artists per refresh, shares a rolling 100-request budget across Discover, Artist News, and Inbox, and keeps successful results in the local database. Configure an appropriate NewsAPI plan for your deployment.

Inbox data refreshes in the background once a day and also refreshes when opened. Artist news advances in the background every six hours and checks due artists when the news page opens.

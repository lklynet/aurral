title: Player
description: Lyrics, radio, the full screen player, and a queue that follows you between devices.
---

Aurral plays your Library in the browser. Play any track, album, artist, or playlist, and the player bar stays at the bottom while you browse. Minimize it to the sidebar when you want the room.

## Queue, lyrics, and friends

**Queue**, **Lyrics**, and **Friends** share a panel on the right. Open it from the strip at the right edge of the page, and it stays open while you browse. Drag tracks in **Queue** to reorder them.

Lyrics come from the track's tags, an `.lrc` file beside it, or LRCLIB. Synced lyrics follow along as the track plays. When LRCLIB has lyrics for a new download that has none, Aurral saves them as an `.lrc` file beside it in the Library folder, so other players that read the folder show them too.

## Full screen

Open the full screen player from the player bar, or press **F**. It shows the cover, lyrics, the queue, and a visualizer. With **Immersive mode** on in **Settings > Appearance**, its colors come from the cover.

## Radio and autoplay

Select **Start radio** on a track, album, or artist to play similar music from your Library. When the queue ends, **Autoplay** keeps going with music like what you just played. Turn it off in **Queue**.

## Pick up on another device

Aurral saves your queue and position on the server. Pause on one device and a paused player on another picks up where you left off. A device that's playing is never interrupted. Subsonic clients like Feishin and Symfonium share the same queue. Previews and tracks outside your Library stay on the device that played them.

## Volume leveling

Turn on **Volume leveling** in **Settings > Listening** to play every track at a similar loudness.

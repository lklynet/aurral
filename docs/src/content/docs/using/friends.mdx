---
title: Friends
description: See what friends are playing, their top plays, and your Blend with them.
---

Friends see what you're playing and your top plays from the last 30 days, and you see theirs. People on your server show up on their own. Friends on other servers need an aurral.org account.

Open **Friends** in the panel on the right. Each row shows what a friend is playing, or what they last played and when. Select one to open their top artists, albums, and tracks, and your Blend with them.

## People on your server

People on your server show up in **Friends** marked **Local**, with no aurral.org account needed. Selecting one also shows a playlist of the 50 tracks they played most in the last 30 days, such as **mia's tracks**. Save it or your Blend to keep it in sync.

You see someone only while you both have **Share listening on this server** on in **Settings > Profile**. Turn it off and people here stop seeing what you play. A saved copy of someone's tracks keeps them and stops syncing once you can't see them.

## Friends on aurral.org

1. Select **Link** in **Settings > Profile**, then sign in on aurral.org with GitHub or Discord and approve the code.
2. In **Friends**, add people by their aurral.org handle. They accept from their own Aurral.

Friend requests, and friends accepting yours, show in your Inbox within 15 minutes. Select a handle on a public playlist, in **Friends**, or in a playlist's members to open that person's aurral.org profile: their public playlists, and for friends what they're playing and their top plays. Add, accept, or remove friends there or in **Settings > Profile > Friends**. **View profile** in **Settings > Profile** shows your own.

## Blends

Your Blend with someone mixes your plays with theirs, using only tracks in your Library. When you don't have a track they play, it picks another by the same artist. **Made for you** on Discover shows Blends with up to 3 people, the ones you share the most artists with. See [Made for you](/using/discover/).

## Privacy

Turn off **Share with friends** in **Settings > Profile** and aurral.org deletes what it holds, so friends stop seeing what you play. A removed friend stops seeing what you play, though their Aurral can keep your tracks in their Blend for up to a day. The admins of your friends' servers can see what you share with them.

An admin can turn off linking, friends, and new public playlists in **Settings > Users**.

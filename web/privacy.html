Privacy

Updated November 16, 2026

Aurral runs on your own server, and your Library, listening history, and settings stay there. This page covers aurral.org, which the Aurral maintainers run. Aurral works fully without it.

Accounts

An aurral.org account is optional. If you make one, we keep:

  • Your account ID from GitHub or Discord. Not your name, email, avatar, or tokens.
  • The handle you pick, shown on playlists you publish.
  • Sign-in sessions, without your IP address or browser.
  • Public playlists: the title and tracks, shown with your handle, until you make the playlist private or unlink.
  • Your friends and the friend requests you've sent, until either of you removes the other.
  • For your friends only: the track you're playing, until it ends, and your top artists, albums, and tracks from the last 30 days, replaced daily. Never your full history. The admins of your friends' Aurral servers can see it too.

Deleting your account removes all of this right away. Your handle stays reserved for 90 days so nobody can pose as you, then anyone can claim it.

Linked servers

When you link an Aurral user to your account, we keep that user's name and a random ID for the server, and show the name on your account page. Linking gives aurral.org no access to your server. Whoever runs that server can act as you through the link, so only link servers you trust. Unlink anytime from your account page or from Aurral.

Share links

When you share from Aurral, aurral.org keeps what you shared (names, MusicBrainz IDs, and whether files can be played) and enough to reach your server: a random server ID, a hash of its secret, and its tunnel address.

Update checks

Aurral asks aurral.org about new releases. We count these checks per hour by version and release channel. Nothing identifies your server.

Hosting

aurral.org runs on Cloudflare, which handles every request, including your IP address, to serve and protect the site. We don't sell or share data, and we don't use ads or trackers.

Contact

Questions or requests go to GitHub issues. If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.

Discovery that shows its work. Built from what you listen to, with a real reason for every pick.

  • Start from your history

    Bring your Spotify history, your existing library, ListenBrainz, or Koito.

  • Adjustable depth

    Safer, balanced, or deeper cuts. Block artists or genres you never want to see.

  • Trends

    Global and local charts, tags, and fresh releases in one place.

  • Preview before you grab

    Hear track previews, top songs, and music videos right on the artist page.

How Discover works

Playlists that refill themselves. Pick the vibe and the schedule. Aurral builds the tracklist, grabs the files, and cleans up after itself.

  • Rotating flows

    Pick the mix, the size, and the days. Aurral does the rest.

  • Made for you

    Daily mixes and Blends from what you play. Nothing downloads until you save one.

  • Bring your playlists

    Import from Spotify, YouTube Music, Last.fm, ListenBrainz, or an M3U file.

  • Play them anywhere

    In the browser, any Subsonic app, or Navidrome, Plex, and Jellyfin.

Playlists and flows

One Inbox for your artists. New releases, shows, and news as they come in.

  • Your own feed

    Releases, shows, news, and friend requests. Read, save, or dismiss each one.

  • Out now, out soon

    Albums from your library artists from the last 30 days and the next 90.

  • Artist news

    Stories from your RSS feeds that mention your library and recommended artists.

  • Shows nearby

    Upcoming concerts for your library artists when you connect Ticketmaster.

Up in 60 seconds.

One container, with a built-in fallback downloader ready out of the box and lossless clients whenever you connect them. Run docker compose up -d, open port 3001, and stop babysitting a five-app music stack.

Setup guide Filesystem and mounts
docker-compose.yml 
services:
  aurral:
    image: ghcr.io/lklynet/aurral:latest
    restart: unless-stopped
    ports:
      - "3001:3001"
    environment:
      - PUID=1000
      - PGID=1000
    volumes:
      - ./config:/config
      - ./data:/data

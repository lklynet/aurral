Recommendations from your own listening. Built from your library and listening history.

  • Your history

    Your library plus Last.fm, ListenBrainz, or Koito history.

  • Adjustable depth

    Safer, balanced, or deeper. More like this, less like that.

  • Trends

    Global trends, tags, and recent releases in one place.

  • Concerts

    Nearby concerts for the artists you already love.

How Discover works

Playlists that refill themselves. Flows mix discovery and your library on a schedule, then fetch the tracks.

  • Rotating flows

    Pick the mix, the size, and the days. Aurral does the rest.

  • Release Radar

    Adopt discover playlists and keep them fresh every week.

  • Bring your playlists

    Import from Spotify, YouTube Music, Last.fm, or ListenBrainz.

  • Play them anywhere

    In the browser, or synced to Navidrome, Plex, and Jellyfin.

Playlists and flows

One Inbox for your artists. New releases, shows, and news as they come in.

  • One Inbox

    Releases, shows, news, and discoveries. Read, save, or dismiss each one.

  • Out now, out soon

    Albums from your artists from the last 30 days and the next 90.

  • Artist news

    Stories from your feeds that mention the artists you love.

  • Shows nearby

    Concerts for your library, recommended, and trending artists.

Inbox and news

Themes. Built-in themes, your own themes, and colors that follow the music.

  • Themes

    Catppuccin, Rosé Pine, Nord, Gruvbox, and more, light or dark.

  • Bring a scheme

    Create a theme, or import any base16 or base24 scheme.

  • Immersive mode

    Accents and backgrounds take their color from the cover art.

  • Synced to your account

    Your theme is saved to your account, on every device.

Install with Docker Compose.

Run docker compose up -d, open port 3001, and create your admin account.

Setup guide Filesystem and mounts
docker-compose.yml 
services:
  aurral:
    image: ghcr.io/lklynet/aurral:latest
    restart: unless-stopped
    ports:
      - "3001:3001"
    environment:
      - PUID=1000
      - PGID=1000
    volumes:
      - ./config:/config
      - ./data:/data
